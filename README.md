One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026:
36 unique stories in a classic broadsheet layout — lead story on Kim Jong Un
ordering wider tactical nuclear deployment, a schematic map from Pyongyang
to Cairo, a key-figures strip, and four regional columns. Every story was
first reported on 26 November 2026 (see the DATE CHECK line in the footer).

Entry for the [news-image-20261126 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — play at https://make.fun/r/wkpcknk0.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png` and validates the result: a whole PNG
under 1 MiB and at most 1024×1024 pixels, with its IEND chunk intact.
