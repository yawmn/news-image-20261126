One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026:
36 unique stories on one full newspaper page of pictures, no gaps —
13 illustrated scenes drawn fresh for their stories (3 large leads:
Kim Jong Un orders wider tactical nuclear deployment, Macy's 100th
parade postponed, Cairo tanker fire toll rises to 31; plus 10 smaller
scenes: Bank of Korea hikes to 3.25%, India marks 26/11, Gukesh–
Sindarov Game 3, Ukraine downs 88 of 112 drones, oil jumps as Hormuz
fears spread, SpaceX gets FCC nod for 1M space data centres, Congo
Ebola cases pass 12,000, Gaza winter storm threatens 1.76M, EU plans
a €50bn tech fund, Nikkei and Kospi rebound), a world map drawn fresh
for this edition with 13 pins numbered to match the illustrated
stories, a dense numbered briefs list for stories 14–36, a UTC
timeline, a key-figures strip, a region bar, a date-check list naming
the stories that are NOT 26 November, and a HOW SURE legend with a
confidence chip on every illustrated story. Every story happened or
was first reported on 26 November 2026. A full source ledger
(`traceability-ledger.json`) records the source and the image location
of every story.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) inspects the bundled
`entry.png` (whole PNG, IEND intact, under 1 MiB, at most 1024×1024
pixels) and delivers it to `/output/entry.png` through a temporary
file and an atomic rename, then reads the written file back and
compares its SHA-256 digest with the bundled one. `--check` inspects
without writing; `--self-test` delivers to a temporary directory.
