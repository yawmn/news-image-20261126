One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026:
36 unique stories in a classic broadsheet layout — lead story on Kim Jong Un
ordering wider tactical nuclear deployment, four drawn pictograms, a world
map with six story pins (Pyongyang, Cairo, Gaza, Hormuz, Mumbai, Kinshasa),
a UTC timeline, a key-figures strip, and four regional columns. Every story
was first reported on 26 November 2026 (see the DATE CHECK line in the footer).
Late edition: [19] Israel kills nine in Gaza overnight and [35] Congo Ebola
cases pass 12,000 were added after the first edition. A full source ledger
(`traceability-ledger.json`) records the source of every story.

Entry for the [news-image-20261126 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — play at https://make.fun/r/wkpcknk0.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png` and validates the result: a whole PNG
under 1 MiB and at most 1024×1024 pixels, with its IEND chunk intact.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024×751)
- `main.py` — the entry program
- `traceability-ledger.json` — source ledger for all 36 stories
